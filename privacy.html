Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: your data stays on your device. I don't run servers, create accounts, or collect your data.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following in a local database on your device, only so it can work:

  • Workouts, sets, exercises, plans and templates that you enter
  • Body weight entries
  • Optional profile details (sex, age, height) used for calorie estimates
  • App preferences, such as units and timer settings
  • Heart rate summaries (average, max, min) attached to a workout

This information is not sent to me or to any third party by the app.

Apple Health

If you allow it, the app reads your heart rate from Apple Health during a workout and saves completed strength-training workouts and estimated active energy to Apple Health. You choose what to allow and can change it at any time in the Health app. Health data is not used for advertising or marketing and is not shared with anyone.

Bluetooth heart rate monitors

If you pair a Bluetooth heart rate strap, the app uses Bluetooth to receive heart rate readings during a workout. Readings are kept on your device. The app may keep the Bluetooth connection active in the background during a workout so readings aren't interrupted.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local notifications on your device to tell you when a rest timer ends. No notification data leaves your device.

Backups, exports and sharing

You can export your data to a file and import it again. Where that file goes (Files, iCloud Drive, email, another app) is your choice, and it is then handled under that service's own privacy policy. The app can also generate a text prompt for you to copy into an AI chat service of your choice to help create a training plan. Nothing is sent unless you paste it yourself, and that service's policy applies to what you share.

What I don't collect

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools
  • No advertising and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites
  • No sale or sharing of personal information

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices and deleting data

Because data is stored only on your device, you control it. Deleting the app removes its data. You can remove or change Health, Bluetooth and notification access at any time in iOS Settings. Data already saved to Apple Health can be deleted in the Health app.

Not medical advice

Calorie and heart rate figures are estimates for general fitness use and are not medical advice.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the effective date above. If the app ever starts collecting data, I'll update the policy before it does.
