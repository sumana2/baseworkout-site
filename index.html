Frequently asked questions

How do I back up or move my data?

Go to the Config tab > Data Management and export a backup file. Save it to Files or iCloud Drive. On a new device, install the app and import that file from the same screen. There is no account or cloud sync, so a backup is the only way to move your data.

What happens to my data if I delete the app?

Everything is stored only on your device, so deleting the app deletes your data. Export a backup first if you want to keep it.

Why does the app ask for Apple Health access?

baseworkout can read your heart rate during a workout to show average, max and min heart rate and to improve the calorie estimate. It can also save your completed strength-training workouts to Apple Health. Both are optional. You can change access any time in Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > baseworkout.

My heart rate wasn't imported.

Watches sometimes sync to Health a few minutes after the workout. Open the finished session and use the import button to try again. Make sure heart rate read access is turned on for baseworkout in the Health app.

How do I connect a Bluetooth heart rate monitor?

Go to Config > Heart Rate Monitor, turn on Bluetooth access when asked, and pick your strap from the list. It must support the standard Bluetooth heart rate profile. This feature is optional and works on iPhone only.

Rest timer alerts don't show when the app is in the background.

Allow notifications for baseworkout in Settings > Notifications. Also check that Focus modes or Silent mode aren't muting the alert.

Are calorie and heart rate figures accurate?

They are estimates for general fitness use. They are not medical advice and are not a diagnostic tool. Talk to a health professional about any medical concern.
